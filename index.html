About E-Waste Management

🌍

What is E-Waste Management?

E-waste management is the safe collection, sorting, recycling and disposal of discarded electrical and electronic equipment — from phones and laptops to appliances and industrial components. Done well, it recovers valuable metals while keeping toxic materials such as lead, mercury and cadmium out of soil, water and air.

📊

Why It Matters

India is one of the world's largest e-waste generators, yet a large share still moves through informal channels. Responsible management protects worker health, reduces environmental damage, strengthens resource security and supports the Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligations that now shape the industry.

🛠️

This Course at VIT

Under Dr. Aniket Kundu's guidance, TE Semester V students turn e-waste data into working products — applying data analysis, visualisation and India's e-waste policy framework to build public dashboards around generation, collection, recycling and city-level performance.

Core Themes

Three Pillars of the Subject

⚖️

E-Waste Rules & Policy

EPR · India

India's E-Waste (Management) Rules and Extended Producer Responsibility framework make producers accountable for take-back, collection targets and safe recycling. Students explore compliance, recycling certificates and the institutions that monitor the flow of electronic waste.

♻️

Recycling & Circular Economy

Circularity

From urban mining and material recovery to repair, reuse and design for disassembly, circular thinking keeps valuable resources in use for longer. Projects examine formal and informal recycling chains and the economics of recovering copper, gold, aluminium and rare-earth materials.

🏙️

State & City E-Waste Analytics

Data · Place

E-waste is generated locally but managed through state and city systems. Students analyse generation estimates, collection infrastructure, recycler capacity and urban performance, then turn those findings into dashboards that make regional gaps and progress visible.
